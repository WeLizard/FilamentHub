# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 1, 2026_

1.1. This Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as the Policy) defines the procedure for processing and protecting personal data of users of the FilamentHub service (hereinafter referred to as the Service), available at filamenthub.ru and filamenthub.club.

1.3. The Policy is developed in accordance with Federal Law No. 152-FZ dated July 27, 2006 “On Personal Data” and other regulatory legal acts of the Russian Federation.

1.4. This Policy is an informational document and does not itself replace a separate consent to personal data processing. Viewing public pages of the Service does not constitute acceptance of the Terms of Service or provision of such consent.

1.5. Personal data about clients and counterparties entered by a User into professional features is processed by the Operator on that User’s instructions under the Terms of Service and is not included in the consent covering the User’s own data.

1.6. Processing is automated. Individual requests may be reviewed by the Operator through Service interfaces and email. The Service does not request citizenship, passport data, special categories of personal data, or biometric data as part of ordinary use.

## 2. Data We Collect

2.1. The following categories of personal data are processed when using the Service:

- Email address, login, and data necessary for registration, sign-in, account recovery, and account security;
- Nickname (username) and an irreversible password hash where a local password is used;
- Display name, where supplied by the selected authentication service or voluntarily entered by the User;
- Profile data provided voluntarily: avatar and the country selected by the User for regional features; the profile country is not treated as citizenship;
- Data provided when applying for brand verification (brand name, corporate email, website, social media links, supporting documents);
- Personal library data and Service settings: spools, printers, material systems, presets, profiles, calculations, and history of related actions;
- Materials published by the User, including reviews, ratings, profiles, and other community content;
- Addressed correspondence with the Operator: sender and recipient addresses, display name where available, subject, message content, attachments, and technical delivery data;
- Records of legally significant actions: accepted document versions, date and time, Account identifier, and technical confirmation data;
- Technical data: IP address, the current network connection country derived from it, user-agent, request date and time, and service identifiers. The connection country is used only to select available sign-in methods and security measures and is not treated as citizenship or profile country;
- Printing settings when using the synchronization function: material presets, printer profiles, print profiles, and technical parameters.

2.2. When a third-party sign-in method is available for the current network connection and the User initiates sign-in through Google or Yandex, we receive the email address, name, and user identifier within the permissions granted.

## 3. Purposes of Data Processing

3.1. Personal data is processed for the following purposes:

- Registration and authorization of the user on the Service;
- Selecting available sign-in methods and registration safeguards based on the current network connection country;
- Providing Service functionality (materials catalog, presets, calculator, OrcaSlicer synchronization);
- Verification of brand and manufacturer representatives;
- Sending service notifications (email confirmation, password reset, system notifications);
- Ensuring Service security and protection against abuse;
- User support, error diagnostics, improving Service quality, and analyzing its operation, including through aggregated metrics.

**3.2. The Operator may send necessary service messages, replies to requests, and other communications permitted by applicable law and the User’s selected settings.**

3.3. Depending on the operation, processing is based on the User’s separate consent, steps requested by the User to enter into or perform the Terms of Service, the Operator’s legal obligations, or another basis provided by law. During OAuth, minimum data is processed to complete the sign-in requested by the User; personal features remain unavailable until the terms and consent are separately confirmed.

## 4. Access Region and Data Location

4.1. The Service determines the current network connection country from the IP address using a local DB-IP Country Lite database. The IP address is not sent to a third party for this lookup. Interface language, time zone, name, and email address are not used for this determination.

4.2. Local email/password sign-in and Yandex sign-in are available regardless of connection country.

4.3. Google authentication is not provided when the connection is from the Russian Federation. It is available only for connections from outside Russia and is unavailable when the connection country cannot be determined. This rule applies both when creating an Account and at each later sign-in.

4.4. Google reCAPTCHA protects standard registration only for connections from outside the Russian Federation. Local abuse controls are used for Russian and undetermined connections.

4.5. Choosing a third-party sign-in service initiates only the minimum processing needed for identification. Until the documents are accepted and consent is provided, the Account remains restricted. The User may delete it immediately; an unfinished registration is automatically deleted after 30 days.

4.6. The Operator is established in the Russian Federation and complies with Russian personal-data law. This Policy does not limit mandatory rights granted to Users outside Russia by applicable law.

4.7. When personal data of Russian citizens is collected, recording, organization, accumulation, storage, correction, and retrieval using databases are performed in the Russian Federation in accordance with Part 5 of Article 18 of Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ.

4.8. The primary Account database and backups containing private User data are located in the Russian Federation. Changing equipment or a Russian infrastructure provider does not change this rule.

4.9. Use of the services listed below does not move the primary Account database. They receive only the data needed for a particular function.

## 5. Third-Party Services

5.1. To ensure the operation of the Service, we use the following third-party services to which certain data may be transferred:

- Google OAuth (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, Ireland / USA) - sign-in through a Google account when this method is available for the current network connection and selected by the User. A request is made to obtain the user’s email and basic profile. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Yandex OAuth (Yandex LLC, Russia) - authorization via Yandex account. A request is made to obtain the user’s email and basic profile. Yandex Privacy Policy: https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/
- Google reCAPTCHA v3 (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, Ireland / USA) - protection against automated requests during standard registration in regions where this service is available. The script is not loaded during ordinary viewing of public pages. Technical data is transmitted for verification. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Yandex Cloud Postbox (Yandex.Cloud LLC, Russia) - delivery of service messages and outgoing addressed correspondence. Delivery requires sender and recipient addresses, an optional display name, subject, content, attachments, and technical delivery data. Yandex Cloud data privacy: https://yandex.cloud/en/security/data-privacy
- Selectel infrastructure (Russia) - hosting of the Service, the primary Account database, backups, and local receipt of incoming correspondence within the Russian data perimeter. Selectel documents: https://selectel.ru/about/documents/
- Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) - content delivery and connection protection for the additional domain filamenthub.club. Requests to that domain pass through Cloudflare nodes, where the connection is decrypted, so this service has access to the visitor’s IP address, technical connection data, and the content of requests and responses on that domain. The primary domain filamenthub.ru is served directly, without Cloudflare. The primary Account database is not hosted with Cloudflare. Purpose: availability of the Service for visitors outside the Russian Federation and reduced load on our hardware by keeping copies of static files. Cloudflare Privacy Policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

5.2. These services process data under the applicable agreements and their published documents. They do not receive data beyond what is necessary for the relevant function. Incoming correspondence is received by the Service’s mail server and stored in the Russian data perimeter.

## 6. Cross-Border Data Transfer

6.1. Cross-border transfer may occur to Ireland and the USA when the User selects Google sign-in or Google reCAPTCHA is used under clauses 4.3–4.4. It also occurs to the USA when the additional domain filamenthub.club is used, because requests to that domain pass through the Cloudflare network; the data is limited to the visitor’s IP address, technical connection data, and the content of requests and responses on that domain. It may also occur when addressed correspondence is delivered to a recipient using a mail system outside the Russian Federation and delivery requires transfer to the country of the recipient or its mail provider. In the latter case, the data is limited to addresses, an optional display name, subject, content, attachments, and technical data needed to deliver that message.

6.2. Cross-border transfer is performed only as required for the selected function and in accordance with applicable law.

6.3. The transfer is not used to host a foreign copy of the primary Account database.

6.4. Google sign-in is optional; local or Yandex sign-in can be used instead. Addressed correspondence is sent abroad only for a specific request or business contact when the destination uses a foreign mail system. Using the additional domain filamenthub.club is also optional: the primary domain filamenthub.ru is served directly, without Cloudflare. Merely visiting public Service pages does not start these operations or constitute consent to a cross-border transfer.

7.4. Limited data may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, and records that must be retained by law, with restrictions on purpose and retention period. Inactive accounts may be deleted after prior notice where reasonably practicable.
