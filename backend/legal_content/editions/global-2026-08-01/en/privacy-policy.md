# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 25, 2026_

## 1. General Provisions

1.1. This Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as the Policy) defines the procedure for processing and protecting personal data of users of the FilamentHub service (hereinafter referred to as the Service), available at filamenthub.ru.

1.2. The personal data operator is Kuzmin I.I., an individual applying the special tax regime “Tax on Professional Income” (NPD taxpayer). Contact email address: support@filamenthub.ru.

1.3. The Policy is developed in accordance with Federal Law No. 152-FZ dated July 27, 2006 “On Personal Data” and other regulatory legal acts of the Russian Federation.

1.4. This Policy is an informational document and does not itself replace a separate consent to personal data processing. Viewing public pages of the Service does not constitute acceptance of the Terms of Service or provision of such consent.

1.5. The Service may be used by individuals who have reached the age of 14 and by legal entities.

## 2. Data We Collect

2.1. The following categories of personal data are processed when using the Service:

- Email address, login, and data necessary for registration, sign-in, account recovery, and account security;
- Nickname (username);
- Full name (only if voluntarily provided by the User);
- Profile data provided voluntarily (e.g., the “about me” field, avatar, and the country selected by the User for regional features);
- Data provided when applying for brand verification (brand name, corporate email, website, social media links, supporting documents);
- Personal library data and Service settings: spools, printers, material systems, presets, profiles, calculations, and history of related actions;
- Materials published by the User, including reviews, ratings, profiles, and other community content;
- Support requests and other correspondence with the Operator, including sender and recipient addresses, message content, delivery metadata, and attachments when submitted;
- Technical data: IP address, the current network connection country derived from it, user-agent, request date and time, and service identifiers. The connection country is used only to select available sign-in methods and security measures and is not treated as citizenship or profile country;
- Printing settings when using the synchronization function: material presets, printer profiles, print profiles, and technical parameters.

2.2. When a third-party sign-in method is available for the current network connection and the User initiates sign-in through Google or Yandex, we receive the email address, name, and user identifier within the permissions granted.

## 3. Purposes of Data Processing

3.1. Personal data is processed for the following purposes:

- Registration and authorization of the user on the Service;
- Selecting available sign-in methods and registration safeguards based on the current network connection country;
- Providing Service functionality (materials catalog, presets, calculator, OrcaSlicer synchronization);
- Verification of brand and manufacturer representatives;
- Sending service notifications (email confirmation, password reset, system notifications);
- Ensuring Service security and protection against abuse;
- User support, error diagnostics, improving Service quality, and analyzing its operation, including through aggregated metrics.

**3.2. The Operator may send necessary service messages, replies to requests, and other communications permitted by applicable law and the User’s selected settings.**

3.3. Depending on the operation, processing is based on the User’s separate consent, steps requested by the User to enter into or perform the Terms of Service, the Operator’s legal obligations, or another basis provided by law. During OAuth, minimum data is processed to complete the sign-in requested by the User; personal features remain unavailable until the terms and consent are separately confirmed.

## 4. Third-Party Services

4.1. To ensure the operation of the Service, we use the following third-party services to which certain data may be transferred:

- Google OAuth (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, Ireland / USA) - sign-in through a Google account when this method is available for the current network connection and selected by the User. A request is made to obtain the user’s email and basic profile. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Messages may be dispatched through infrastructure in Ireland, while certain provider service data is processed in the USA. Resend Privacy Policy: https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy

4.2. These services process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not transfer any data to these services beyond the minimum necessary for the operation of the respective function.

## 5. Cross-Border Data Transfer

5.1. In connection with the use of third-party services (Section 4), necessary data may be transferred to Ireland (EU), the USA, and other jurisdictions where the relevant providers and their processors operate.

5.2. Data transfer is carried out exclusively to the extent necessary to provide Service functionality (authorization, bot protection, delivery of service emails).

5.3. The conditions and legal basis for such transfers are determined with regard to applicable law and the specifics of the relevant function.

5.4. Where a particular operation requires separate User consent or another lawful basis, the Operator obtains it before performing that operation. Use of the Service alone is not treated as separate consent to a cross-border transfer.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

6.1. Personal data is retained no longer than required for the processing purposes, operation of the account, protection of the parties’ rights and legitimate interests, and mandatory legal requirements.

6.2. The User may delete their account independently through the personal account interface or by sending a request to support@filamenthub.ru.

6.3. When an account is deleted, private identifiers and account data are deleted or anonymized in accordance with applicable law. Published community materials may be retained without a link to the deleted account unless otherwise required by law or the User’s rights.

6.4. Limited data may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, and records that must be retained by law, with restrictions on purpose and retention period. Inactive accounts may be deleted after prior notice where reasonably practicable.

## 7. Data Security

7.1. The Operator takes necessary legal, organizational, and technical measures to protect personal data from unauthorized access, destruction, modification, blocking, and other unlawful actions.

7.2. Such measures include access controls, protection of credentials, encryption in transit, and other safeguards appropriate to the nature and risks of processing.

7.3. The Operator maintains the confidentiality of personal data and discloses it only where a lawful basis exists. No method of transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

9.4. The User may disable cookies in browser settings, but authentication and some Service features may then become unavailable.
