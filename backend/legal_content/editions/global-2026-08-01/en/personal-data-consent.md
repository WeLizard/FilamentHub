1.1. This Consent is given for the processing of personal data both with and without the use of automation tools.

1.2. Consent is given for the processing of the following personal data of mine:

- Data provided when applying for verification as a representative of a brand or manufacturer:

  - Corporate email;
  - Official website;
  - Links to the brand’s social media;

The connection country is used only to select available sign-in methods and security measures and is not treated as citizenship or profile country.

4.1. Personal data is processed while the Account is used and afterwards only to the extent necessary for the stated purposes or required by law.

4.2. Consent remains valid until the processing purposes are achieved, the Account is deleted, or consent is withdrawn, whichever occurs first, unless another lawful basis permits or requires continued processing.

4.3. After consent is withdrawn, the Operator stops processing based specifically on consent and deletes or anonymizes the relevant data within the period required by applicable law. Processing may continue only where another lawful basis exists.

## 5. Procedure for Withdrawal of Consent

5.1. Consent may be withdrawn by the personal data subject or the subject’s representative by sending an application to the Operator at the email address: support@filamenthub.ru.

5.2. The application for withdrawal of consent must contain the following information:

- The email address to which the account was registered;
- Nickname (username) or account ID (if available);
- Full name (if previously provided to the Operator);
- The text of the application for withdrawal of consent to personal data processing.

5.3. After receiving the request, the Operator acts under clause 4.3 of this Consent and applicable law. Withdrawal may make continued use of personal Account features impossible.

6.3. The Operator maintains the confidentiality of personal data and limits disclosure to cases with a lawful basis or those necessary for a feature selected by the User.
